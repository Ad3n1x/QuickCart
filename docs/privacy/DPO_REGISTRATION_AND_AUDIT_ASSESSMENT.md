# QuickCart — DPO, major-importance registration and audit assessment

**Status:** High-priority legal classification required. No registration, exemption, DPO appointment or compliance certification is claimed.

## Major-importance assessment

The NDPC GAID 2025 Schedule 7 Guidance Notice says a controller/processor that maintains a filing system and operates in a listed sector such as e-commerce may be designated as a data controller/processor of major importance. It separately identifies a threshold of more than 200 data subjects in six months and commercial ICT services on other people's devices. QuickCart is designed as a buyer/seller marketplace and therefore appears to fall within the e-commerce description; this creates a substantial registration issue even if the platform is early-stage or has not yet reached 200 users.

The precise designation, category/tier, registration fee, deadline and audit-return obligations must be confirmed by a qualified Nigerian DPCO against the current Schedule 7 and QuickCart's actual business model and processing. Do not conclude QuickCart is outside scope solely because it is free at launch, operated by an individual or has low current usage.

Schedule 7 distinguishes UHL, EHL and OHL using factors including data sensitivity, financial assets, cloud reliance, cross-border flows, vulnerability, automation and numbers of data subjects. It identifies payment gateway providers and fintechs as UHL, but that does not automatically make QuickCart itself a payment gateway or fintech. The OHL category includes certain data-volume and sensitive-data criteria. The e-commerce sector trigger and the applicable fee tier must be separately analysed; do not guess the category or fee.

## DPO

NDPA section 32 requires a controller of major importance to designate a DPO with expert knowledge. GAID Articles 11–14 address designation, publication of contact details, position, internal reporting and credential assessment. The DPO may be an employee or engaged under a service contract; the DPO must have the necessary competence, independence, access and resources. A DPO's public contact details must be published and communicated to NDPC as prescribed.

No DPO is currently appointed. Do not publish a fabricated DPO contact or imply that the privacy mailbox is a DPO. If QuickCart is confirmed as a controller of major importance, DPO appointment is a release requirement. A qualified Nigerian DPCO should advise on the appropriate arrangement and the operator's governance/capacity before any agreement or filing is made.

## Individual operator / high-risk processing

GAID Article 34(4) states that where an agreement involves a sole proprietorship, individual agent or self-employed worker who may not be organised like a corporate entity, that person must be trained in data protection and privacy for high-risk processing; evidence of training and registration as a data processor of major importance is described as rebuttable proof of compliance. This provision should be assessed by a qualified DPCO in light of QuickCart's structure and risk. It is not a substitute for the controller's own duties or the DPO/DPIA requirements.

## Audit and filing

- GAID Article 7 expects a controller/processor to conduct an NDP Act compliance audit within 15 months of commencement of business and annually thereafter.
- A major-importance controller/processor must register under section 44 and GAID Articles 8–9.
- UHL/EHL categories file annual Compliance Audit Returns (CAR); OHL has annual registration renewal and is not required to file annual CAR under GAID Article 9(3).
- GAID Schedule 7 classification, actual establishment/commencement date and category determine the relevant fee and deadline. Confirm these against the current NDPC portal and a DPCO; do not assume a fee or due date from an old article.
- A DPO's internal semi-annual data-protection report and continuous professional development/credential rules must be addressed where applicable.

## DPIA

GAID Article 28(3) explicitly lists e-commerce services and cross-border data transfers as mandatory DPIA circumstances. The DPIA must follow Schedule 4, be vetted and signed by a certified DPO accredited by NDPC, and be submitted as required. QuickCart's current draft is preliminary only and does not satisfy this obligation.
