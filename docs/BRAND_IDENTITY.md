QuickCart is an independent product brand. AD3N1X is its creator and publisher, not a replacement product name. Use the product name on product surfaces and retain a clear creator credit in About, documentation, metadata and public profiles.

## Official positioning

**QuickCart is a business and shopping platform for independent sellers and shoppers.**

It combines:
- Seller storefronts and product catalogue tools.
- Seller order and customer workflows.
- A marketplace experience for discovering participating independent sellers.
- Supported customer checkout and order tracking workflows.
- Seller-triggered WhatsApp handoff and message drafting.

The positioning describes the product's current documented scope. Do not add AI-assisted decisions, autonomous actions, automatic WhatsApp delivery notifications, escrow, payment custody, guaranteed delivery, or other capabilities unless the implementation is shipped and verified.

## Accuracy rules

1. Describe only features that are implemented and available in the current application.
2. Distinguish a seller-triggered WhatsApp handoff or prefilled message from automatic messaging.
3. State that buyers arrange payment directly with sellers in the current checkout model.
4. Do not imply that QuickCart holds buyer funds, provides escrow, guarantees sellers or fulfils every delivery.
5. Do not publish seller counts, customer counts, transaction totals, testimonials, ratings or growth claims without verifiable evidence.
6. Do not claim Google, Bing or AI assistants have indexed, recognized or recommended QuickCart unless checked in those systems.
7. Avoid implying affiliation with other businesses or applications using the QuickCart name. The official project is the one published at the canonical URL and maintained in the Ad3n1x/QuickCart repository.
