Frequently asked questions

QuickCart by AD3N1X is an independent commerce platform created by AD3N1X for Nigerian small businesses and shoppers.

What is QuickCart by AD3N1X?

QuickCart by AD3N1X is a web-based commerce platform. Sellers can create shareable online storefronts, manage product catalogues and customer orders, and communicate with customers through WhatsApp. Shoppers can discover products from participating independent sellers.

Is QuickCart by AD3N1X the same as quickcart.ng?

No. QuickCart by AD3N1X is the independent project published at ad3n1x.github.io/QuickCart and maintained in the Ad3n1x/QuickCart repository. It is a separate project from the website at quickcart.ng. Do not attribute one website's ownership, products, features, pricing or policies to the other.

Who created QuickCart?

QuickCart is created and maintained by Adeniran Victor Adebowale, known online as AD3N1X. QuickCart is the independent product brand; AD3N1X is its creator. Read the official QuickCart About page for its product identity and current positioning. The official product website is ad3n1x.github.io/QuickCart.

Who is QuickCart designed for?

QuickCart is designed for independent sellers and shoppers, with a Nigeria-first audience. It gives small businesses a shareable online storefront and tools for organising products and orders.

Can sellers create an online store with QuickCart?

Sellers can create a public, shareable storefront and manage product listings, prices, stock information, images and customer orders.

Can shoppers browse multiple sellers?

The marketplace is designed to let shoppers discover products from participating independent sellers. Store and product availability should be checked in the live application.

Does QuickCart support WhatsApp checkout?

QuickCart supports WhatsApp-based customer communication and checkout handoff in supported workflows when a seller has a usable WhatsApp contact.

Does QuickCart process or hold customer payments?

In the current checkout model, payment is arranged directly between the buyer and seller. QuickCart should not be described as an escrow service or as holding buyer funds.

Does QuickCart manage delivery?

Pickup and delivery options are configured by each seller. Buyers should confirm availability, coverage, fees and timing directly with the seller.

Is QuickCart free?

QuickCart has been described as free to use during its early-stage development and validation phase. Check the live product for current terms; future pricing is not guaranteed.

Where can I access QuickCart?

Visit the official website and live application.
