The product behind the name

Business and shopping, connected.

QuickCart is an independent commerce platform created for Nigerian businesses and shoppers. It brings seller storefronts and practical business tools together with a marketplace where customers can discover participating independent sellers.

What QuickCart is

QuickCart is a web-based business and shopping platform. Sellers can set up a public storefront, organise product listings and manage customer orders. Shoppers can browse participating stores and place orders through the supported shopping flow.

Its product identity is independent: QuickCart is the product; AD3N1X is its creator. The name QuickCart is also used by unrelated products, so the publisher and official website should be checked before attributing information to this project.

What the product currently supports

For sellers

Public storefronts, product catalogue management, product images and stock information, customer and order workflows, and manual order entry.

For shoppers

Marketplace discovery, public seller storefronts, cart and supported checkout flows, and customer-facing order tracking.

Customer communication

Seller-triggered WhatsApp handoff and message drafts for supported order-status workflows. Messages are not represented as automatic WhatsApp delivery notifications.

Seller operations

Store-scoped product and order data, customer records and order-derived dashboard summaries for supported workflows.

Feature availability can depend on the seller's setup and the workflow being used. Check the live application for current availability.

How payments and fulfilment work

In the current checkout model, buyers arrange payment directly with the relevant seller. QuickCart is not presented as an escrow service or as a custodian of buyer funds. Delivery and pickup options, charges, coverage and timing are determined by each seller and should be confirmed with them.
