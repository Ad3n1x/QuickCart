SHOPPING HELP

How shopping on QuickCart works

Discover products, shop across stores and connect with each seller.

Can I shop from different sellers?

Yes. Add products from different businesses to your cart. QuickCart keeps each seller's order separate.

Can I shop without an account?

Guest checkout is available in the live marketplace.

How do payments work?

Payment is arranged directly with the seller. Confirm the payment details with the business before paying.

Who handles delivery?

Each seller controls their available fulfilment options. Confirm delivery coverage and fees with the seller.
