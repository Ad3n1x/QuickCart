# QuickCart Organic Launch Pack

## Core message
Your business. Your store. One link.

## Primary CTA
Create your store.

## 7-day content cycle
1. Problem: Still taking every order through DMs?
2. Demo: Watch me create an online store.
3. Education: 3 reasons customers abandon complicated ordering.
4. Product: Show storefront -> cart -> checkout -> order.
5. Transformation: Before QuickCart vs after QuickCart.
6. Founder story: Why QuickCart was built.
7. Community: Show us your QuickCart store.

## Short-form hooks
- Still answering “How do I order?” all day?
- Your social page is not your checkout.
- Stop sending 12 product pictures when one link can do it.
- From “How much?” to “Order received.”
- Give your customers somewhere simple to shop.
- Small business. Professional storefront.
- Build your store. Share one link. Take orders.

## Seller outreach
Hey! I came across your business and noticed you sell through social media.

I’m working on QuickCart, a simple tool that lets small businesses create their own online store and share one link with customers.

Instead of sending product pictures and answering “how do I order?” repeatedly, customers can browse the store and place an order.

I’d love to set one up with you and get your honest feedback.

## Success metrics
Track:
- Accounts created
- Stores published
- First product added
- Store links shared
- First orders
- Returning sellers

Prioritize first orders over likes and followers.
