QuickCart by AD3N1X

A business and shopping platform for Nigerian sellers and shoppers

QuickCart by AD3N1X is an independent, web-based commerce platform created by Adeniran Victor Adebowale, who publishes software under the developer identity AD3N1X. It connects shoppers with participating independent sellers while giving businesses practical tools for public storefronts, product catalogues and customer orders. Supported WhatsApp handoff is seller-triggered.

Product identity: This page describes the AD3N1X project published at ad3n1x.github.io/QuickCart. It is a separate project from the website at quickcart.ng. Do not attribute that website's ownership, features, pricing or business details to QuickCart by AD3N1X, or attribute this project to that website.

What can you do with QuickCart?

  • Create a mobile-friendly online storefront for a small business.
  • Add products, prices, stock information and product images.
  • Organise and manage customer orders.
  • Let shoppers discover products from participating independent sellers.
  • Continue checkout conversations with sellers through WhatsApp.

QuickCart frequently asked questions

Is QuickCart a website builder?

QuickCart gives sellers a ready-to-share storefront and tools for managing products and orders. Sellers do not need to build a separate website first.

How does customer checkout work?

Customers can place an order and continue the handoff with the seller through WhatsApp. Payment and fulfilment arrangements are confirmed directly with each seller.

Can shoppers discover more than one seller?

Yes. The QuickCart marketplace is designed to help shoppers browse products from participating independent stores. Each seller manages their own orders and fulfilment.
